# SMS Manual Part 4 - Drills and Emergency Preparedness

Document SMS-04 | Revision 6 | Applies to all Coralis Shipping managed vessels

## 4.1 Responsibility

The Master is responsible for ensuring that all drills are carried out at the required frequency and recorded. The Chief Officer plans the monthly drill schedule and the Safety Officer keeps the drill records in the Official Log Book and in the electronic Drill Register (form SMS-F-041).

## 4.2 Drill Frequencies

- Abandon ship drill: every month. Each crew member must take part in at least one abandon ship drill per month.
- Fire drill: every month, held together with or immediately after the abandon ship drill.
- Enclosed space entry and rescue drill: at least once every 2 months.
- Man overboard drill: every 3 months.
- Oil pollution (SOPEP) drill: every 3 months.
- Steering gear emergency drill: every 3 months.
- Damage control drill (passenger and cargo vessels above 500 GT): every 6 months.
- Ship Security drill: every 3 months, and within 1 week of a crew change affecting more than 25% of the crew.

If more than 25% of the crew have not taken part in an abandon ship and fire drill on board that ship in the previous month, both drills must be held within 24 hours of the ship leaving port.

## 4.3 New Crew Members

New crew members must receive safety familiarisation before the vessel sails, and in any case within 24 hours of joining. Familiarisation covers muster stations, lifejacket and immersion suit donning, alarm signals and escape routes, and is recorded on form SMS-F-012 signed by the Safety Officer and the crew member.

## 4.4 Conduct of Drills

Drills should be realistic and varied: the scenario, location and simulated casualty should change from drill to drill. Lifeboats must be lowered to the water and manoeuvred at least once every 3 months. Rescue boats must be launched and manoeuvred in the water every month where reasonable and practicable. Each drill ends with a debrief within 2 hours, and lessons learned are entered on form SMS-F-043.

## 4.5 Records and Reporting to the Office

Drill records are sent to the DPA (Designated Person Ashore) with the monthly safety report by the 5th day of the following month. A missed or postponed drill must be reported to the DPA within 48 hours with the reason and the rescheduled date.

## 4.6 Emergency Equipment Checks

- Lifejackets and immersion suits: inspected monthly by the Safety Officer.
- Fixed CO2 system: inspected annually by an approved service provider.
- EEBDs (emergency escape breathing devices): inspected monthly; replaced at the end of their 10-year service life.
- Fire extinguishers: inspected monthly; serviced annually; hydrostatically tested every 10 years.
