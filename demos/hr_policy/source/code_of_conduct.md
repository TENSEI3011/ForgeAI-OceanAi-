# Coralis Shipping Pvt. Ltd. - Code of Conduct for Shore Staff

Policy code: HR-POL-02 | Version 3.0 | Effective 1 January 2025 | Owner: Human Resources and Compliance

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Code sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all shore-based employees in the Mumbai and Singapore offices, including fixed-term employees and interns. Breaches of the Code are handled under the disciplinary procedure in Section 9. Every employee must complete the online Code of Conduct training in PeoplePort within 30 days of joining and then once every year.

## 2. Working Hours and Hybrid Work

The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 9 hours per day including a 1-hour lunch break. Core hours, when all staff must be available, are 10:00 to 16:00. Employees may start work at any time between 08:00 and 10:00.

After completing probation, employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with their line manager's approval. Staff on the 24-hour vessel support desk follow their published roster instead of these hours.

## 3. Dress Code and Site Safety

Business casual dress is expected from Monday to Thursday and smart casual on Fridays. When visiting a vessel, shipyard or port terminal, staff must wear personal protective equipment: safety shoes, a helmet and a high-visibility vest. A Port Visit Permit must be obtained from the Marine Operations team at least 2 working days before any port or vessel visit.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept a gift from a business partner only if its value does not exceed USD 50 (INR 4,000 or SGD 70) per occasion. Any gift above this value must be declared in the Gifts Register in PeoplePort within 5 working days and may only be kept with approval from the Compliance Officer. Cash or cash equivalents (vouchers, gift cards) must never be accepted.

Business hospitality such as a meal is acceptable up to USD 100 per person per event, provided there is a clear business purpose. No gifts or hospitality of any value may be accepted from a supplier that is taking part in an active tender with the company.

When employees offer hospitality to clients while travelling, the limits and approval rules of the Travel and Expenses Policy (HR-POL-03, Section 9) apply.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare any actual or potential conflict of interest (for example a family member working for a supplier, or a personal investment in a competitor) within 30 days of it arising, using the Conflict of Interest form in PeoplePort. In addition, every employee must submit an annual declaration by 31 January each year, even if they have nothing to declare.

## 6. Confidentiality and Data Protection

Company, customer and crew information must be handled according to its classification (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted). Confidential or Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Any suspected data breach, lost laptop or phishing incident must be reported to IT Security at security@coralis-shipping.example within 24 hours of discovery.

## 7. Use of IT and Social Media

Company IT equipment is for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work. Employees must not post confidential information, vessel positions or customer names on social media, and must not speak on behalf of the company publicly unless authorised by Corporate Communications.

## 8. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Coralis Shipping has zero tolerance for bribery. Facilitation payments are prohibited, including small payments to port or customs officials to speed up routine actions. Any request for such a payment must be refused and reported to the Compliance Officer within 24 hours.

## 9. Disciplinary Procedure

Misconduct is handled in stages:
1. Verbal warning, recorded in PeoplePort.
2. Written warning, which remains valid for 12 months.
3. Final written warning, which remains valid for 24 months.
4. Termination of employment.

Gross misconduct, including fraud, theft, harassment, violence, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs at work, and unauthorised absence of 3 or more consecutive working days (see Leave Policy HR-POL-01, Section 11), may lead to suspension on full pay pending inquiry and to dismissal without prior warnings. A disciplinary inquiry must be completed within 30 days of the suspension. The employee has the right to be accompanied at any disciplinary hearing by a colleague of their choice.

## 10. Grievances and Speak-Up

An employee with a work-related grievance should first raise it with their line manager. If it is not resolved within 10 working days, the employee may escalate it in writing to their HR Business Partner, who must respond within a further 10 working days.

Concerns about unethical or illegal conduct can be raised confidentially, and anonymously if preferred, through the Speak-Up hotline on 1800-209-4455 or speakup@coralis-shipping.example. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.

## 11. Prevention of Harassment

The company does not tolerate harassment of any kind. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee, which must be contacted within 3 months of the incident. The Internal Committee completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving a complaint.

## 12. Resignation and Notice Periods

Employees who resign must give written notice in PeoplePort:
- During probation: 15 days.
- Confirmed staff: 60 days.
- Managers at grade M3 and above: 90 days.

Annual leave may not be used to shorten the notice period unless the Department Head approves it in writing; unused leave is otherwise encashed as described in the Leave Policy (HR-POL-01, Section 3). Company property, including laptops and access cards, must be returned on the last working day.
