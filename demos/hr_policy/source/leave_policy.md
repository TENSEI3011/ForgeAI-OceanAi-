# Coralis Shipping Pvt. Ltd. - Leave Policy for Shore Staff

Policy code: HR-POL-01 | Version 4.2 | Effective 1 January 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out the leave entitlements of shore-based employees of Coralis Shipping Pvt. Ltd. working in the Mumbai head office and the Singapore branch office. It applies to permanent and fixed-term employees. It does not apply to seafarers serving on company-managed vessels, whose leave is governed by the Crew Employment Agreement, or to contractors supplied by agencies.

All leave is applied for, approved and recorded in the PeoplePort HR system. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. General Principles

Leave must be planned in advance with the line manager so that operations, especially vessel support desks, remain covered. A manager may decline or reschedule leave for genuine business reasons, but must give the reason in PeoplePort within 3 working days of the request. Approved leave can only be cancelled by the company in an emergency, and any non-refundable travel costs incurred by the employee are then reimbursed under the Travel and Expenses Policy (HR-POL-03).

## 3. Annual Leave

Employees with less than 5 years of continuous service are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per leave year. Employees with 5 or more years of continuous service are entitled to 24 working days. Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.75 days per month (2 days per month for the 24-day entitlement).

During the first 6 months of employment (the probation period), leave accrues normally, but no more than 5 days of annual leave may be taken.

Notice requirements:
- For annual leave of 3 or more consecutive working days, apply in PeoplePort at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave.
- For 1 or 2 days of annual leave, apply at least 2 working days in advance.

Carry forward: a maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of that year; any carried-forward days not used by 31 March lapse automatically.

Leave encashment: unused annual leave is not paid out during employment. On resignation or termination, up to 30 days of accrued but unused annual leave are encashed at the basic rate of pay. Annual leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without the written approval of the Department Head (see Code of Conduct HR-POL-02, Section 12).

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Unused sick leave is not carried forward and is not encashed.

An employee who is unwell must inform their line manager by phone or message before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for any absence of more than 2 consecutive working days and must be uploaded to PeoplePort within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave beyond 12 days is treated as leave without pay, unless the employee chooses to use annual leave instead.

## 5. Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per leave year for short personal matters. Casual leave may be taken for a maximum of 2 consecutive days and cannot be combined with annual leave on either side. Casual leave does not carry forward.

## 6. Maternity and Paternity Leave

Maternity leave: employees in the Mumbai office are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Employees in the Singapore office are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave. The employee should notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start of maternity leave.

Paternity leave: employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

Adoption leave: an employee adopting a child under 12 months of age is entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

## 7. Bereavement and Marriage Leave

Bereavement leave of 5 working days is granted on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law), and 2 working days on the death of a grandparent or grandchild.

Marriage leave of 5 working days is granted once during employment, to be taken within 1 month of the wedding date.

## 8. Public Holidays

HR publishes the holiday calendar for each office every December for the following year. The Mumbai office observes 12 public holidays per year and the Singapore office observes the 11 gazetted public holidays of Singapore. Staff on duty rosters (for example the 24-hour vessel support desk) who work on a public holiday receive compensatory time off under Section 10.

## 9. Leave Without Pay and Sabbatical

Leave without pay of up to 30 calendar days may be granted for personal reasons once annual leave has been exhausted. It requires approval from both the Department Head and the HR Business Partner. Employees with 7 or more years of service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 6 months, approved by the Director of Human Resources.

## 10. Compensatory Time Off

An employee who works on a weekly off-day or a public holiday at the manager's request earns 1 day of compensatory time off ("comp-off") for each such day worked. Comp-off must be recorded in PeoplePort within 7 days and availed within 60 days of the day worked, after which it lapses. Business travel on weekends also earns comp-off, as set out in the Travel and Expenses Policy (HR-POL-03, Section 6).

## 11. Unauthorised Absence

Absence without approval or without informing the manager is unauthorised absence and is unpaid. Unauthorised absence of 3 or more consecutive working days is treated as a serious disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct (HR-POL-02, Section 9).
