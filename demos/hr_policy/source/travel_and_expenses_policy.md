# Coralis Shipping Pvt. Ltd. - Travel and Expenses Policy

Policy code: HR-POL-03 | Version 2.4 | Effective 1 January 2025 | Owner: Finance and Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers business travel and work-related expenses of shore-based employees of the Mumbai and Singapore offices. Travel for seafarers joining or leaving vessels is arranged by Crewing and is outside the scope of this policy.

## 2. Travel Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved in the PeoplePort Travel module before any booking is made:
- Domestic travel is approved by the line manager.
- International travel is approved by the Department Head.
- Any trip longer than 10 days, or with an estimated total cost above USD 5,000, also requires approval from a Director.

Travel requests must be submitted at least 7 days before departure for domestic travel and at least 21 days before departure for international travel. Late requests need a written justification from the approver.

## 3. Booking and Air Travel

All flights and hotels are booked through the company travel desk (Orbit Travels), contactable at travel@coralis-shipping.example. Bookings made privately are reimbursed only if they cost no more than the travel desk's quote.

Class of travel:
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class only for flights longer than 9 hours, and only for managers at grade M3 and above.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to the following nightly limits, excluding taxes:
- Tier A cities (Singapore, London, Rotterdam, Dubai, Hamburg): USD 220 per night.
- Tier B cities (Mumbai, Chennai, Kuala Lumpur, Busan): USD 150 per night (INR 12,500 in India).
- All other locations: USD 110 per night.

Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel is allowed and attracts a flat allowance of USD 30 per night.

## 5. Per Diem

A daily allowance covers meals and incidentals; separate meal receipts are not required:
- International travel: USD 60 per day.
- Domestic travel in India: INR 2,500 per day.

On the departure day and on the return day, 75% of the per diem applies. If a meal is provided free (for example by a client or a conference), the per diem is not reduced.

## 6. Weekend Travel and Compensatory Time Off

Employees who are required to travel on a weekend or on a public holiday earn 1 day of compensatory time off for each such day spent travelling. The comp-off must be recorded in PeoplePort and availed within 60 days, as set out in the Leave Policy (HR-POL-01, Section 10).

## 7. Ground Transport

Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed for business journeys. Airport transfers by taxi are allowed at both ends of the trip. Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per km in India and SGD 0.60 per km in Singapore, on submission of a mileage log. Car hire abroad needs Department Head approval.

## 8. Expense Claims and Advances

Expense claims must be submitted through PeoplePort Expenses within 30 days of the end of the trip, with itemised receipts for every item above USD 25 (INR 2,000). Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip are not reimbursed unless a Director approves them. Approved claims are reimbursed by bank transfer within 15 working days of approval.

A cash advance of up to 70% of the estimated per diem and hotel costs may be requested for international trips. Advances must be settled with an expense claim within 10 days of returning.

## 9. Client Hospitality While Travelling

Employees may host clients for a business meal while travelling up to USD 100 per person, consistent with the Code of Conduct (HR-POL-02, Section 4). Hospitality above this amount needs prior approval from the Department Head. The names of guests and the business purpose must be recorded on the claim.

## 10. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol (except within approved client hospitality), hotel minibar items, in-room movies and personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, seat or class upgrades paid by the employee, and costs of family members travelling with the employee.

## 11. Corporate Cards

A corporate credit card is issued to employees who travel more than 4 times a year. Card transactions must be reconciled in PeoplePort within 7 days of the monthly statement date. Personal use of the corporate card is not allowed.

## 12. Travel Insurance and Emergencies

All approved business trips are covered by the company's corporate travel insurance. In a medical or security emergency abroad, contact Global Assist on +65 6800 1122 (24 hours), then inform your line manager.
