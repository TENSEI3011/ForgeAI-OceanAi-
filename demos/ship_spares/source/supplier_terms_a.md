# Anchorline Marine Supplies - General Terms of Supply

Supplier ID: SUP-A | Terms version 2025-1 | Valid for orders placed from 1 January 2025 | Warehouses: Rotterdam and Singapore

## 1. Application

These terms apply to all purchase orders placed by Coralis Shipping fleet procurement with Anchorline Marine Supplies (supplier ID SUP-A) for items listed against SUP-A in the shared spares catalogue. Where a purchase order and these terms conflict, these terms prevail unless Anchorline confirms the change in writing.

## 2. Prices and Price Validity

Catalogue prices are quoted in US dollars, excluding freight and local taxes, and remain valid until 31 March 2026. A volume discount of 5% applies when 10 or more units of the same part number are ordered on one purchase order. The volume discount is applied before any early-payment discount.

## 3. Payment Terms

Payment is due net 30 days from the invoice date. An early-payment discount of 2% of the invoice value is granted when payment is received within 10 days of the invoice date. Overdue invoices attract interest of 1.5% per month.

## 4. Minimum Order Value

The minimum order value is USD 500 per purchase order. Orders below USD 500 are accepted but carry a small-order handling fee of USD 45.

## 5. Dispatch and Delivery

- In-stock items are dispatched within 2 working days of order confirmation.
- Delivery to vessels or agents in Singapore or Rotterdam takes 5 working days from order confirmation.
- Delivery to all other ports takes 7 to 10 working days from order confirmation.
- Items that are out of stock are delivered within the catalogue lead time (lead_time_days) plus 3 days for consolidation and inspection.

Express service: in-stock items can be dispatched within 24 hours for a surcharge of 15% of the order value, with a minimum surcharge of USD 120.

## 6. Freight

Freight is free for orders above USD 2,500 delivered to Singapore or Rotterdam. Below that value, and for other destinations, a flat freight charge of USD 85 per order applies, except for items over 150 kg, which are quoted separately.

## 7. Warranty

Standard warranty is 12 months from delivery or 6,000 running hours, whichever comes first. Pumps and pump parts (PU- series, for example PU-2200 ballast pump impellers) carry an extended warranty of 18 months. Warranty claims must be notified within 14 days of discovering the defect, with photographs and running-hour records.

## 8. Returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery, subject to a restocking fee of 10% of the item value. Electrical items (EL- series) cannot be returned once the packaging has been opened. Returns require a Return Authorisation number from Anchorline before shipping.

## 9. Certificates and Documentation

Certificates of conformity are provided free of charge on request. Class approval certificates for safety equipment (SF- series) cost USD 35 per certificate. Butterfly valves VL-3102 are supplied with a class certificate included in the price.

## 10. Part-Specific Notes

- ME-1042 (fuel injector nozzle set, product tanker engines) is supplied as a set of 2 nozzles; the catalogue price is per set.
- SF-7000 series immersion suits are packed in cartons of 4; orders are rounded up to full cartons.
- For NV-8000 series gyro compass sensor modules a service engineer visit can be booked at USD 950 per day plus travel.

## 11. Late Delivery

Anchorline does not pay penalties for late delivery, but will refund the express surcharge in full if an express shipment is dispatched later than promised.
