# Bluefin Ship Spares - Terms and Conditions of Sale

Supplier ID: SUP-B | Edition 7 | Effective 1 February 2025 | Warehouses: Piraeus and Fujairah

## 1. Scope

These terms govern sales by Bluefin Ship Spares (supplier ID SUP-B) to Coralis Shipping for all catalogue items listed against SUP-B. Bluefin specialises in main engine, electrical and deck machinery spares.

## 2. Quotations, Prices and Discounts

Prices are in US dollars, ex-warehouse. Quotations and catalogue prices are valid for 60 days from the quotation date. A volume discount of 8% applies to the whole order when the order value before discount exceeds USD 10,000. There is no discount for quantities of a single part number.

## 3. Payment Terms

Payment is due net 45 days from the invoice date. No early-payment discount is offered. For orders with a value above USD 20,000, an advance payment of 50% is required before dispatch, with the balance due net 45 days.

## 4. Minimum Order

The minimum order value is USD 300. There is no small-order fee, but freight is always charged as described in Section 6.

## 5. Dispatch and Delivery

- In-stock items are dispatched within 3 working days of order confirmation.
- Delivery to vessels or agents in Piraeus or Fujairah takes 4 working days from order confirmation.
- Worldwide delivery by air freight takes 10 to 14 working days from order confirmation.
- Out-of-stock items are dispatched within the catalogue lead time (lead_time_days).

Bluefin does not offer a standard express service. For a vessel that cannot operate until a part arrives, the Vessel-Not-Operational (VNO) priority service guarantees dispatch within 24 hours for a surcharge of 25% of the order value.

## 6. Freight and Handling

Freight is charged at the carrier's cost plus a handling fee of 8% of the freight cost, for every order and every destination.

## 7. Warranty

Standard warranty is 6 months from delivery. Main engine parts (ME- series, for example ME-1100 main bearing shells and ME-1140 fuel pump plungers) carry a 12-month warranty. An extended warranty can be purchased at 4% of the item price for each additional 6 months, up to a maximum total warranty of 24 months.

## 8. Returns

Unused items may be returned within 14 days of delivery, subject to a restocking fee of 15% of the item value. Made-to-order items, defined as items with a catalogue lead time above 30 days (for example the EL-5140 shore connection cable reel), cannot be returned.

## 9. Late-Delivery Penalty

If Bluefin delivers later than the confirmed delivery date, Coralis Shipping may deduct a penalty of 1% of the order value for each day of delay, capped at 10% of the order value.

## 10. Part-Specific Notes

- NV-8020 series ECDIS display units include software updates for 24 months from delivery.
- ME-1120 cylinder liner O-ring kits must be stored below 25 C; storage instructions are included in each box.
- Orders for EL-5100 series circuit breakers must state the switchboard maker and serial number.
